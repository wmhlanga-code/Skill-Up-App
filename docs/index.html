How It Works

Hire a professional
in 4 easy steps

No calls, no hassle. Browse providers near you, read real reviews, and book instantly right from the app.

1

Open the App

Sign up and allow location access. SkillUp immediately shows skilled providers in your area.

2

Browse & Filter

Filter by category, distance, rating, or price. View full profiles with services, reviews, and availability.

3

Book or Message

Send a booking request or start a chat directly in-app. WhatsApp shortcut available too.

4

Review & Repeat

After the job, leave a review. Save favourites for next time. Build a trusted circle of local pros.

Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Live Map View

See providers pinned on a real-time map with their category, distance, and availability status at a glance.

Ratings & Reviews

Verified reviews from real customers. Providers build reputations over time — you always know who you're hiring.

In-App Messaging

Real-time chat between seekers and providers, with WhatsApp shortcut for those who prefer it.

AI Assistant

Powered by Groq. Helps seekers find the right provider; helps providers write their bio and set pricing.

Booking System

Providers receive booking requests with job details. Accept or decline with one tap — no back-and-forth needed.

Push Notifications

Instant alerts for new messages, booking requests, and status updates so nothing falls through the cracks.

Favourites

Save your best providers for quick access next time. Build a personal list of trusted local professionals.

Dark Mode

Fully supported light and dark themes that follow your device preference automatically.

For Everyone

Built for three types
of users

Service Seeker

Need something done? Find, vet, and hire the best local professional in minutes.

  • Browse nearby providers on a map
  • Filter by category, distance & rating
  • Send booking requests instantly
  • Chat or WhatsApp providers directly
  • Save favourites for quick re-hiring
  • Leave reviews after completed jobs

Service Provider

Skilled professional? Reach more clients in your area and grow your business.

  • Create a verified profile with services
  • Toggle availability on/off anytime
  • Accept or decline booking requests
  • Dashboard: stats, views, ratings
  • AI assistant for bio & pricing help
  • Real-time messages with clients

Business Owner

Running a service business? List your company and get verified exposure to local customers.

  • Business profile with verification
  • Showcase all services in one place
  • Manage bookings and enquiries
  • Build credibility with reviews
  • Appear in category search results
